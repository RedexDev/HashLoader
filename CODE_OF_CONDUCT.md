# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

HashLoader is a community project for people interested in HashLink, Haxe, Rust, game modding, and related tools. We want participation to be welcoming and constructive, whether you are reporting a bug, asking a question, sharing a mod, or contributing code or documentation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and considerate of different experience levels and viewpoints.
- Give feedback about ideas and code, not personal characteristics.
- Keep discussions focused, useful, and relevant to HashLoader and its community.
- Explain disagreements constructively and accept that maintainers may make decisions different from your suggestions.
- Respect the rights, privacy, and work of others, including game developers and other projects.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, insults, personal attacks, threats, doxxing, sexualized attention, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable. Do not publish someone else's private information or use project discussions to promote abusive, illegal, or harmful activity.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies in HashLoader GitHub issues, pull requests, discussions, and other spaces when someone is representing the project or its community.

## Enforcement

The project maintainer is responsible for clarifying and enforcing this code. They may remove or edit content, close discussions, or limit participation depending on the severity and context of a violation. If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact the maintainer privately through GitHub rather than escalating the situation publicly.

This code of conduct applies to all project participants, regardless of their role or contribution history.
